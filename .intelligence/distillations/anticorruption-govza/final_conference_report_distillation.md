## 🔍 Forensic Findings & Systemic Pathologies

### 📌 Finding ID: WB-2025-001 (Incentive Benchmarking)
- **Vulnerability**: High personal risk/ruin for whistleblowers discourages reporting.
- **Pathology**: Lack of financial safety net for "corruption resistors."
- **Solution**: Awarding a percentage (e.g., 10%) of recovered assets to contribution to successful convictions.

### 📌 Finding ID: WB-2025-002 (Reverse Burden of Proof)
- **Vulnerability**: Whistleblowers struggle to prove retaliation is linked to their disclosure.
- **Pathology**: Labour law imbalance favoring employers in PDA cases.
- **Solution**: Reversion of the burden of proof to the employer in retaliation cases.

### 📌 Finding ID: WB-2025-003 (Sextortion Awareness)
- **Vulnerability**: Women coerced into "sex for marks" or "sex for jobs" are victims of a specific corruption type.
- **Pathology**: Gender-blind anti-corruption policies.
- **Solution**: Gender-responsive policies and recognition of sextortion as corruption.

### 📌 Finding ID: WB-2025-004 (The "Impipi" Pathology)
- **Vulnerability**: Historical stigma from the apartheid era demonizes reporting.
- **Pathology**: Cultural barrier to "heroic" whistleblowing.
- **Solution**: Behavioral change campaigns to glorify whistleblowers as "Ubuntu" heroes.

## 📉 Strategic Resolutions (2025 Conference)
1. **Independent Agency**: Establish a body led by a retired judge, potentially within the OPI framework.
2. **Legislative Consolidation**: Amending the PDA to include third parties, media, and investigators.
3. **Citizens' Bill**: Civil society to draft a bill to amplify citizen voices in Parliament.
4. **Funding Mechanism**: Tapping into CARA and Public-Private Partnerships (PPP) with tax incentives.
