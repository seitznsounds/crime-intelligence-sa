## 🎯 Executive Summary
The NACAC Final Report represents the definitive strategic pivot for South Africa’s anti-corruption architecture. Its core proposal is the creation of the **Office of Public Integrity (OPI)**—a Chapter 9 institution designed to "neutralize" organized crime networks and resolve the "Reporting Void" (currently at 55% non-reporting). It explicitly acknowledges institutional resistance from SAPS and DOJ&CD.

## 🚩 Key Intelligence Nodes

### 1. The "OPI" Solution (Architecture)
*   **Constitutional Shield**: Must be entrenched in Chapter 9 to prevent the "Scorpions" fate (political disbandment).
*   **Binding Recovery**: Recommendations from OPI audits to be binding and enforceable by court order.
*   **Whistleblower Protector**: Proposed retired judge as a dedicated "Whistleblower Protector of South Africa."

### 2. The "Reporting Void" Data
*   **Metric**: Only **45%** of South Africans would report corruption.
*   **Driver**: Fear of community retaliation and lack of trust in SAPS.
*   **Economic Impact**: State capture "tainted" R57bn (Zondo) but actual losses estimated at **R1.5 trillion** (4% GDP growth impact).

### 3. Institutional Resistance
*   **Obstruction**: NACAC noted significant difficulty in engaging with **SAPS** and **DOJ&CD**.
*   **Independence Gap**: NPA is currently a "programme" of the DOJ&CD, compromising financial and personnel independence.
