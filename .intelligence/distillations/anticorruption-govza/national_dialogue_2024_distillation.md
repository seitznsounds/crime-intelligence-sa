## 🎯 Executive Summary
The 2024 National Dialogue (held Dec 2024) served as a "Codesa moment" for anti-corruption, uniting the Presidency, NACAC, and Public Service Commission. The core outcome is a mandate for an "All-of-Society" approach, with youth and women positioned as central change agents. It identifies public procurement as the "existential threat" to service delivery and endorses the OPI as the primary fix.

## 🚩 Key Intelligence Nodes

### 1. Legislative "Must-Haves"
*   **PDA Expansion**: Must cover contractors/3rd parties, not just employees.
*   **Reverse Onus**: Shift burden of proof to employers in retaliation cases.
*   **Whistleblower Support Fund**: Financial/psychological/legal assistance.

### 2. Frontline Procurement Corruption
*   **Sanitation/Water**: Contractors in informal settlements soliciting bribes from residents for "free" services.
*   **Asivikelane Model**: Community-led tracking of municipal records (High-fidelity data source).

### 3. Institutional Challenges
*   **NPA Dedicated Unit**: Proposal for a permanent unit within NPA for complex state capture cases.
*   **Professionalization**: Mandatory ethics training (NSG) is a success but faces "mindset resistance" from older civil servants.
