# Forensic Intelligence Distillation: PPLAAF Activity Report 2017

## 🛡️ Metadata
- **Source**: PPLAAF (Platform to Protect Whistleblowers in Africa)
- **Period**: 2017 - Early 2018
- **Focus**: State Capture (South Africa), BGFI Bank Scandal (DRC), Police Fraud (Nigeria).
- **Status**: High-Fidelity Intelligence (Primary Case Studies)

## 🎯 Intelligence Points: South Africa State Capture

### 1. Whistleblower Profile: Mosilo Mothepu
- **Role**: Former CEO, Trillian Financial Advisory.
- **Intelligence**: Documented the "removal of Finance Ministers" due to Gupta influence.
- **Reprisal Mechanism**: Sued for cyber-crime, fraud, and theft. Lawsuits effectively prevented her from finding new work.
- **Action**: Testified to Parliament (Portfolio Committee on Public Enterprises) in Oct 2017.

### 2. Whistleblower Profile: Bianca Goodson
- **Role**: Former CEO, Trillian Management Consulting.
- **Intelligence**: Revealed criminal collusion between McKinsey and Trillian to raid Eskom funds.
- **Reprisal Mechanism**: Fired by Sage (new employer) immediately after public disclosure despite prior transparency.
- **Action**: Released detailed statement and affidavit through PPLAAF.

### 3. Entity Analysis: Trillian Capital / Salim Essa
- **Type**: Private "Conduit" for State Capture.
- **Mechanism**: Used connections to land Eskom contracts, then handed them to external partners (like McKinsey) for massive kickbacks.
- **Seizure**: AFU seized R95 million in Jan 2018.

### 4. Entity Analysis: McKinsey South Africa
- **Type**: Global Consulting Firm.
- **Corruption**: Received illegal payments from Eskom via Trillian.
- **Seizure**: AFU seized R1.1 billion in Jan 2018 (Proceeds of Crime).

### 5. Data Asset: GuptaLeaks
- **Volume**: 540,000 pieces of information (bank transfers, contracts, emails).
- **Core Revelation**: Systemic hijacking of South African SOEs (State-Owned Entities).

## 🎯 Intelligence Points: DRC / Lumumba Papers

### 1. Whistleblower Profile: Jean-Jacques Lumumba
- **Role**: Senior Executive, BGFI Bank RDC.
- **Intelligence**: Misuse of US$42.9 million from Central Bank of Congo.
- **Reprisal**: Threatened with a firearm by the bank director (Kabila's adopted brother).

### 2. Entity Analysis: BGFI Bank / EGAL
- **Corruption**: Used Central Bank funds to import wild animals (antelopes, giraffes) for President Kabila's private farm (Ferme Espoir).
- **Mechanism**: Unsecured loans and tax cuts granted to "officially" food-importing companies (EGAL).
