## 🚩 Key Intelligence Nodes

### 1. The "PIC Pattern" (South Africa)
*   **Modus Operandi**: CEO (Dan Matjila) using internal disciplinary measures and police resources to hunt whistleblowers rather than investigate allegations.
*   **Scale**: PIC manages R2 trillion+ (primarily government employee pensions).
*   **Retaliation**: Suspension and firing of CIO (Mayisela) and Corporate Secretary (Mathebula) for cooperating with authorities.

### 2. The Fishrot Scandal (Namibia/Angola)
*   **Volume**: 30,000 emails/docs (WikiLeaks).
*   **Corruption**: Samherji (Iceland) bribed Namibian ministers for horse mackerel quotas.
*   **Outcome**: Resignation of Fisheries and Justice ministers.

### 3. The "Oran Port" Scandal (Algeria)
*   **Corruption**: Fraudulent invoices and fee avoidance benefiting Ali Haddad's ETRHB.
*   **Legal Trap**: Tounsi faced complaints for "disclosing proprietary business secrets" — highlighting the tension between corporate secrecy and public interest.

## ⚖️ Legislative Gaps Identified
*   **Proprietary Secrecy as Shield**: Using trade secret laws to prosecute whistleblowers (Algeria).
*   **Police as Weapon**: Using SAPS/Police to identify sources (Mayisela case) instead of investigating crimes.
*   **Bailiff Transcription**: The use of bailiffs to transcribe recordings of threats (Coulibaly case) as a tactical evidence-gathering method.
