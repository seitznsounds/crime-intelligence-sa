INTEGRITY PLEDGE
I, __________________________________, pledge to uphold the highest standards of integrity in all
aspects of my life. Recognizing the importance of ethical conduct in fostering a just and transparent
society, I commit to the following principles:
1. Honesty: I will consistently strive to be honest and transparent in all my actions, interactions,
and communications, refraining from misleading or deceptive practices.
2. Accountability: I accept responsibility for my actions and decisions, acknowledging the
consequences they may have on others. I will be open to scrutiny and willing to be held
accountable for my conduct.
3. Fairness and Equity: I will treat all individuals with fairness, equality, and respect, valuing
diversity and inclusivity while refraining from discrimination and bias.
4. Resisting Corruption: I will reject all forms of corruption, bribery, and unethical behaviour. I
pledge to report any instance of corrupt practices without fear or favour.
5. Ethical Decision-Making: I commit to making decisions based on ethical considerations,
following the law, and adhering to moral principles even when faced with challenges.
6. Promoting a Culture of Integrity: I will actively work towards fostering a culture of integrity
and ethical behaviour in my environment, encouraging, and supporting others to do the same.
7. Continuous Improvement: I pledge to continuously educate myself on ethical standards,
seeking opportunities for personal growth and development in ethical decision-making and
behaviour.
8. Supporting Transparency: I will support efforts to enhance transparency in all sectors of
society, advocating for open and accessible information to ensure accountability.
I understand that upholding this integrity pledge is an ongoing commitment, and I pledge to abide by
these principles in all my personal and professional endeavours. I am aware of the impact my actions
can have on others and the broader society, and I commit to being a responsible and ethical individual.
