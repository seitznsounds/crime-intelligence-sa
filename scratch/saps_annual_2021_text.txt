Property-related Crime refers to crimes during which tangible property of an individual or institution
is taken by criminal elements without permission, and in the absence of the owner/custodian of such
property.
This type of crime includes:
 Burglary at residential premises
 Burglary at non-residential premises
 Theft of motor vehicle and motor cycle
 Theft out of or from motor vehicle
 Stock theft
