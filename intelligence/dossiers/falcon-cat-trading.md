# Dossier: Falcon Cat Trading and Suppliers

## Case ID: FC-SUP-2026
**Status:** HIGH-RISK HUB
**Parent Entity:** Big Five Cartel
**Primary Beneficial Owner:** Vusimuzi 'Cat' Matlala

## Profile Summary
Falcon Cat Trading and Suppliers is a primary logistics and procurement vehicle for the 'Big Five Cartel'. It has been identified as a key node for funnelling government tender proceeds into cartel operations and political events.

## Key Findings
- **Beneficial Ownership:** Closely linked to Vusimuzi Matlala. Intelligence suggests Matlala uses the entity to manage logistics for both legitimate security contracts and illicit syndicate activity.
- **Government Contracts:** Identified as one of three Matlala-linked suppliers (alongside Black AK and Cor Kabeng) receiving significant payments from state tenders.
- **Physical Hub:** Operational base in Ekurhuleni, Gauteng, serving as a transit point for "VIP Protection" equipment which often masks illicit transport.

## Forensic Links
- **Lead Operative:** Vusimuzi 'Cat' Matlala.
- **Financial Vehicle:** Used for 'lobbying' payments to KZN-based political branches.
- **Source:** Madlanga Commission testimony and deep web-browser forensics (`shell_audit_falcon_cat_trading.json`).

## Strategic Role
Falcon Cat provides the "legitimate" front required for the cartel to operate within the South African economy, securing the cash flow necessary for large-scale operations like the 'Port Shepstone Cocaine Trail'.
