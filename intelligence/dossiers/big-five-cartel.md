# Dossier: Big Five Cartel

## Profile Summary
The **Big Five Cartel** is a sophisticated criminal syndicate operating primarily in Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. It is characterized by its "hybrid" nature, combining traditional organized crime (narcotics, hits) with white-collar tender fraud and high-level political influence.

## Risk Score: 98/100
The cartel represents an existential threat to the South African constitutional order through its successful "criminal capture" of the justice and security sectors.

## Key Operations
- **Narcotics:** Linked to the 2021 Port Shepstone cocaine theft and large-scale distribution networks.
- **Tender Fraud:** Systematic manipulation of government contracts in SAPS, Health, and Transport departments.
- **Assassinations:** Responsible for high-profile hits on whistleblowers (Babita Deokaran), business rivals (DJ Sumbody), and uncooperative officials.
- **Judicial Interference:** Allegedly maintains a "payroll" of court officials and judges to ensure immunity for its members.

## Leadership & Key Figures
- **Vusimuzi 'Cat' Matlala:** Primary leader (currently detained).
- **Katiso 'KT' Molefe:** Enforcer and operations manager.
- **Jonathan 'King Mswazi' Msibi:** Late "Senior President" and tactical architect.

## Investigator Narrative
The Big Five Cartel has evolved beyond a gang into a shadow governance structure. By infiltrating the SAPS and the NPA, they have neutralized the state's ability to respond to their crimes. The 'The Green Lounge' project was intended to be their crown jewel—a private gateway for illicit trade protected by the facade of a legitimate development. The Madlanga Commission's exposure of their methods is the first significant challenge to their dominance in years.
