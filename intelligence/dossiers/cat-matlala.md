# Dossier: Vusimuzi 'Cat' Matlala

## Profile Summary
Vusimuzi 'Cat' Matlala, also known as 'John Wick' in underworld circles, is a Pretoria-based tenderpreneur and the alleged leader of the **Big Five Cartel**. He owns several security and trading companies, most notably **Cat VIP Protection Services**, which has been implicated in irregular government contracts across South Africa.

## Risk Score: 96/100
Matlala represents a significant threat to national security due to his systemic infiltration of the South African Police Service (SAPS) and his ability to influence political appointments.

## Key Allegations & Evidence
- **Tender Fraud:** Masterminded the R360 million SAPS Medicare 24 contract fraud.
- **Political Capture:** Allegedly channeled millions of rands into ANC political events via middleman Brown Mogotsi to secure protection from the Ministry of Police.
- **Violence & Intimidation:** Implicated in multiple attempted murders, including a 2023 hit on his ex-girlfriend Tebogo Thobejane.
- **Whistleblower Assassination:** Linked to the network that targeted Babita Deokaran after she exposed irregular contracts at Tembisa Hospital.

## Current Status
- **Detention:** Currently held at eBongweni C-Max in Kokstad, South Africa's most secure prison.
- **Legal:** Trial for Medicare 24 fraud is pending. Facing additional charges for conspiracy to murder and corruption.

## Associated Entities
- Cat VIP Protection Services
- Falcon Cat Trading and Suppliers
- Black AK Trading
- The Green Lounge Project (Proposed R70m private terminal)

## Investigator Narrative
Cat Matlala is not merely a businessman but a central node in a vast criminal-political nexus. His control over 'The Green Lounge' project was a strategic attempt to create a sovereign illicit channel at OR Tambo International Airport. His detention has caused a power vacuum in the Big Five Cartel, leading to increased volatility among his associates.
