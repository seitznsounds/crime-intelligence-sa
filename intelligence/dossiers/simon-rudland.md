# Dossier: Simon Rudland

## Profile Summary
Simon Rudland is the co-founder of **Gold Leaf Tobacco Corporation (GLTC)** and a central figure in regional gold smuggling and money laundering operations. Based primarily in Zimbabwe and South Africa, he is alleged to be a primary bankroller of the "Gold Mafia."

## Risk Score: 95/100
Rudland's operations represent a multi-billion dollar drain on the regional economy, facilitated by high-level state capture in Zimbabwe.

## Key Allegations & Evidence
- **Gold Smuggling:** Allegedly uses GLTC's cash reserves to purchase gold on the black market in Zimbabwe, which is then smuggled to Dubai.
- **Money Laundering:** Utilizes complex corporate structures to "clean" proceeds from illicit gold and tobacco sales.
- **State Influence:** Maintains significant influence over Zimbabwean state institutions, including the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe, to facilitate currency and gold movement.

## Current Status
- **Investigation:** Primary target of international money laundering investigations following the "Gold Mafia" exposé.
- **Sanctions Risk:** Facing potential international sanctions due to his role in undermining the regional financial system.

## Associated Entities
- Gold Leaf Tobacco Corporation (GLTC)
- Fidelity Printers and Refiners (Zimbabwe)
- Gold Mafia Syndicate

## Investigator Narrative
Simon Rudland operates at the intersection of legitimate industry and large-scale illicit trade. By leveraging the tobacco industry's high cash volumes, he has created a self-sustaining cycle of gold smuggling. His ability to operate with relative immunity in Zimbabwe makes him a "protected" actor whose operations are critical to the survival of the Big Five Cartel's broader regional interests.
