# Dossier: Katiso 'KT' Molefe

## Profile Summary
Katiso 'KT' Molefe is a high-profile businessman and suspected underworld kingpin. He is alleged to be a senior enforcer for the **Big Five Cartel**, specializing in operational logistics, hit squad management, and judicial interference.

## Risk Score: 94/100
Molefe represents a high risk due to his direct involvement in violent crime and his demonstrated ability to manipulate court processes to avoid detention.

## Key Allegations & Evidence
- **Mastermind of DJ Sumbody Murder:** Accused of ordering and coordinating the 2022 hit on Oupa John Sefoka (DJ Sumbody).
- **Judicial Bribery:** Allegedly paid R2.5 million to court officials at the Alexandra Magistrate's Court to secure bail in October 2025, bypassing a High Court ruling.
- **Underworld Coordination:** Acts as a bridge between legitimate business interests and violent street-level gangs used for "enforcement" tasks.

## Current Status
- **Legal:** Currently out on bail, which is under review following the Madlanga Commission's exposure of the alleged bribe.
- **Investigation:** Subject of an intensified probe by the Hawks' Serious Organised Crime unit.

## Associated Entities
- Big Five Cartel
- Alexandra Magistrate's Court (Site of bail controversy)
- Various "lifestyle" businesses used as fronts

## Investigator Narrative
KT Molefe is the "muscle" that makes the Big Five Cartel's high-level corruption possible. While Matlala handles the political and financial capture, Molefe ensures that rivals and whistleblowers are silenced. His recent bail controversy has become a symbol of the 'criminal capture' of the justice system, making his eventual re-arrest a high-priority objective for the NPA.
