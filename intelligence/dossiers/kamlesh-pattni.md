# Dossier: Kamlesh Pattni

## Profile Summary
Kamlesh Pattni, also known as "Brother Paul," is a Kenyan-born businessman and self-styled pastor. He rose to infamy during the **Goldenberg Scandal** in Kenya and has recently emerged as a key facilitator in the Zimbabwean gold smuggling network.

## Risk Score: 92/100
Pattni's expertise in large-scale financial fraud and his ability to replicate successful criminal models across different jurisdictions make him a high-priority target.

## Key Allegations & Evidence
- **Gold Mafia Facilitation:** Filmed offering to use his diplomatic and political connections to smuggle gold and launder money for undercover investigators.
- **Goldenberg Scandal:** Orchestrated a scheme that cost Kenya an estimated 10% of its GDP in the 1990s through fake gold and diamond exports.
- **Religious Front:** Uses his "religious" status as a pastor to build trust and move funds through charitable and church organizations.

## Current Status
- **Active Operations:** Continues to operate out of Zimbabwe and Dubai, facilitating high-value transactions for regional syndicates.
- **Legal History:** Despite numerous investigations, Pattni has historically avoided long-term imprisonment through legal maneuvers and political protection.

## Associated Entities
- Goldenberg International
- Gold Mafia Network
- Various religious front organizations

## Investigator Narrative
Kamlesh Pattni is a master of "structural corruption." He does not just break the law; he attempts to capture the law-making and law-enforcement processes themselves. His recent involvement in the Gold Mafia suggests that the Big Five Cartel is leveraging his historical "expertise" to professionalize their money laundering operations.
