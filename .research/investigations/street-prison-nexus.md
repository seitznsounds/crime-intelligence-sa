## Overview
Analysis of the Jonny Steinberg monograph "Nongoloza's Children" reveals a critical historical shift in the late 1980s where prison gang lore (26s, 27s, 28s) spilled onto the streets of the Cape Flats. Today, major street gangs like 'The Americans' and 'The Firm' are fully integrated with the prison Number system.

## Key Nodes
- **The Americans:** Large street gang linked to the 26s; focused on commercial crime and narcotics.
- **The Firm:** Street syndicate linked to the 28s; focused on traditional protection rackets and territorial control.
- **Colin Stansfield (Late):** Former 28s leader and 'gangster capitalist' who blurred the line between crime and political patronage.
- **Jackie Lonti (Late):** Former 26s leader; key figure in bringing the Number lore to the streets.
