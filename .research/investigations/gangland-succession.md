# Investigation: Gangland Succession & State Infiltration (2025-2026)

## Overview
A major reconfiguration is underway in the South African underworld following the assassinations of legacy leaders. This track monitors the power vacuum, the rise of a "new guard," and continued state collusion.

## Key Succession Nodes
- **The 28s Alliance:** Consolidation under **Ralph Stanfield** following the death of **Ernie 'Lastig' Solomon**.
- **The Americans:** Power struggle after the death of **Igsaan 'Sanie American' Davids**. **Sadia Madatt** identified as a rising figure in Rocklands.
- **KZN Drug Hubs:** The **Bloods Gang** (Periasamy family) and **Tatu Gang** (Anthony Gounden) competing for dominance in Phoenix and Verulam.

## State Infiltration Alerts
- **Athlone SAPS:** Allegations of a police sergeant soliciting bribes and smuggling drugs into Pollsmoor.
- **Sophiatown SAPS:** Accused by survivors of the Westbury shooting of working with the **Varados** and **Fast Guns**.
- **Western Cape Anti-Gang Unit:** 10 members denied bail for the murder of **Wade Price** during interrogation.

## Leads to Follow
- Map the beneficial ownership of tow-truck businesses used as fronts in Phoenix/uMhlanga.
- Investigate the links between the **Junior Mafias** and the **Nice Time Kids (NTK)** in Salt River.
- Track the movement of "Operation Prosper" (SANDF deployment) and its impact on hit-style killings.
- Audit the "B97 route" conflict between **CATA** and **CODETA**.
