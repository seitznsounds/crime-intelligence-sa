# Investigation: Transnational Syndicate Hubs (Triads & Sicilian Mafia)

## Overview
Cape Town supergangs (Hard Livings, The Americans) have established deep operational links with international syndicates for specialized smuggling operations.

## Key Nodes
- **Sun Yee On / Wo Shing Wo:** Hong Kong triads control the abalone poaching pipeline from the Western Cape.
- **Sicilian Mafia:** Historical links to diamond smuggling and cannabis trafficking to Europe.
- **The Americans:** Linked to Triads and Nigerian Mafia for narcotics distribution.
- **Hard Livings:** Known to have cooperated with the Sicilian mafia in illegal diamond trafficking.

## Leads to Follow
- Identify current "Dragonheads" of Sun Yee On operating in Johannesburg and Cape Town.
- Map the "Abalone for Drugs" barter system between local poachers and Asian syndicates.
- Investigate the 2000-era gross income from abalone (est. $32m) and trace current 2026 equivalents.
- Search for connections between the Israeli Mafia (Abergils) and local ecstasy distribution networks.
