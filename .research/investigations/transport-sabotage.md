# Investigation: Strategic Transport Sabotage (Taxi Wars & Rail Arson)

## Overview
The South African taxi industry (minibus taxis) carries over 60% of commuters. Evidence suggests systematic sabotage of competing public transport (specifically Metrorail) to ensure high demand for taxi services.

## Key Nodes
- **CATA / CODETA:** Major taxi associations in the Western Cape with a history of violent route conflicts.
- **Santaco:** National taxi body.
- **Ricardo Khan:** Convicted arsonist who linked train torching to taxi rank employment.

## Leads to Follow
- Map the correlation between "well-organized" train arson incidents and taxi route disputes.
- Identify the hitmen/operatives hired by associations for "route clearance" operations.
- Investigate the 1998 case where 13 police officers were charged with complicity in taxi violence.
- Track current 2026 routes under dispute (e.g., B97 route between Bellville and Paarl).
