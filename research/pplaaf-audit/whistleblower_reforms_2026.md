## 🏛️ Executive Summary: The "Dual Home" Crisis
The intelligence confirms a systemic "Governance Limbo" within South Africa's primary justice bodies. The friction between the **Department of Justice and Constitutional Development (DOJCD)** and the **National Prosecuting Authority (NPA)** is not merely bureaucratic—it is lethal.

### Key Findings:
1.  **Structural Sabotage**: The NPA remains administratively and financially subordinated to the DOJCD. The Director-General of Justice acts as the NPA's accounting officer, effectively giving the executive a "chokehold" over prosecutorial resource allocation.
2.  **The WPU Limbo**: The Witness Protection Unit (WPU) is trapped in a "dual home" arrangement—legally in DOJCD, operationally in NPA. This results in leadership vacancies, funding gaps, and a lack of psychosocial aftercare, contributing to the premature abandonment of the programme and subsequent assassinations of witnesses.
3.  **The 2026 Legislative Shield**: Two major bills (Protected Disclosures Bill 2026 and Public Procurement Amendment Bill 2026) represent a radical shift toward **Financial Incentives** (15-25% rewards) and **Direct Witness Protection**.
4.  **Institutional Delay**: The proposed **Office of Public Integrity (OPI)**—a Chapter 9 body designed for independence—is being "deferred" and "pending" review, suggesting political resistance to a truly independent oversight body.
