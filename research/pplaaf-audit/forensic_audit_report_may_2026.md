### 2. The "Section 7 Reporting Loop" (WPA Pathology)

### 3. The "Restitution Gap" and SLAPP Fragility
The **Protected Disclosures Act (PDA)** is restricted by a "12-24 Month Salary Cap" for compensation. This fails to address the "Financial Ruin" lifecycle, which includes industry-wide blacklisting and "Social Ostracism." Furthermore, South African law lacks a statutory defense against **SLAPP Suits** (Spurious Civil Litigation), used by capture-era enablers to bleed whistleblowers of legal resources.

### 4. Legislative "Chilling Effects" (POSI & Cybercrimes)
The pending **Protection of State Information (POSI) Bill** lacks a public-interest defense, threatening whistleblowers with 25-year sentences for "classified" leaks. Combined with the **Cybercrimes Act** and **Sec 205 of the Criminal Procedure Act**, the state maintains a "Secrecy Shield" that can be weaponized to compel journalists to reveal sources.
