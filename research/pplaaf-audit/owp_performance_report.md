# Forensic Report: The Collapse of the Office for Witness Protection (1998-2026)

## 📊 Executive Summary
This report analyzes the performance of the **Office for Witness Protection (OWP)** in South Africa, tracing its decline from a world-class unit in the early 2000s to a state of **"Lethal Governance Limbo"** by 2026. Despite the robust powers granted by the **Witness Protection Act 112 of 1998**, the system has failed the state's most critical whistleblowers.

## 🏛️ The "Dual Home" Crisis
The OWP suffers from a structural pathology known as the "Dual Home" crisis:
*   **Legal/Financial Home**: Department of Justice (DOJCD).
*   **Operational Home**: National Prosecuting Authority (NPA).
*   **Result**: A "Nowhere Home" phenomenon where witnesses are trapped in safe houses for years because the DOJCD lacks the budget for permanent relocation, and the NPA lacks the mandate for civil "aftercare."
